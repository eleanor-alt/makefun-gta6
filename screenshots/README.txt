Screenshots of Palmira Heat, taken by its maker from the game itself, land here
(3 or 4 PNG or JPEG files; the first by name is the cover). The scenes can be posed
with the game's own staging switch, for example:

  node serve.ts, then open
    http://localhost:8080/?shot=chase   a three-star pursuit with a job on the HUD
    http://localhost:8080/?shot=pier    the Corsairs at dusk
    http://localhost:8080/?shot=drive   the coast road in a stolen Solstice
    http://localhost:8080/?shot=foot    downtown on foot
