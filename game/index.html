PALMIRA HEAT

An open-world crime game in a sun-soaked bay city. Steal the cars, run the jobs, dodge the law — and be back at Dee's Diner before the coffee gets cold.

Marlo Reyes used to be the best wheelman on the coast. One diner, one old friend, and four jobs stand between Marlo and a quiet life.

Keyboard and mouse. Your progress saves at the diner and after every job.
